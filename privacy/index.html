Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026Operator: Logan Tierno Lauerpeel.support@gmail.com
The short version: Pomise shares only the information needed for an accepted promise. Private Screen Time selection tokens, browsing history, content viewed, and app-by-app activity are not placed in shared promise records. Pomise does not sell personal information or use advertising or cross-app tracking.

1. Who operates Pomise

Pomise is operated by Logan Tierno Lauer, an individual developer in the United States. In this policy, “Pomise,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the Pomise app and its operator. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to peel.support@gmail.com.

2. What Pomise does

Pomise helps people make screen-time promises with friends. Depending on the chosen format, one or both participants privately select apps through Apple's Screen Time controls. Pomise shares the accepted agreement, aggregate progress, and results needed for accountability.

3. Information used by the app

Profile and identity

Pomise uses a display name, fruit character, and an account identifier derived from the person's iCloud account to connect a participant to accepted promises. The underlying CloudKit user record name is transformed before it is stored in Pomise's promise data.

Promise information

A shared promise may contain requested app names, promise format, limit, duration, scheduled start, time zone, consequence, agreement revisions, acceptance and readiness decisions, aggregate usage checkpoints, daily pass, fail, or unverified results, grace-day status, technical-review information, and consequence-completion status.

Screen Time information

Apple's Family Controls picker provides private, opaque selection tokens. Those tokens, the selected applications and categories, and app-by-app activity stay on the measured device. Pomise's shared records use only the requested app names entered into the agreement, combined progress checkpoints, and promise results. Pomise does not receive or share what content a person viewed or which individual app produced a checkpoint.

On-device information

Private Screen Time selections, monitoring configuration, local notification state, blocked-account identifiers, prepared safety reports, and pilot analytics are stored on the device. The pilot analytics log records product-flow events but excludes names, account identifiers, app lists, consequence text, technical-report notes, exact usage, contacts, and Screen Time tokens. It is not uploaded automatically.

Support information

If someone contacts support, we receive the email address, message, attachments, and other information they choose to provide. Pomise never asks for an Apple Account password, Screen Time passcode, bank credentials, or opaque Family Controls tokens.

4. How information is used

We use information to provide profile setup, promise creation and invitations, agreement and counteroffer flows, verification, aggregate progress, results, technical review, consequence follow-through, rematches, synchronization, safety support, requested notifications, and troubleshooting. We may also use support information to investigate abuse, security incidents, or service failures.

Pomise does not sell or rent personal information. The pilot has no advertising, data-broker integrations, or tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Pomise does not currently process payments, wagers, bank information, or charitable transfers.

5. When information is shared

  • Accepted promise partners: The creator and accepted participant can see the shared agreement, identities used in that promise, aggregate checkpoints, results, technical-review status, and consequence status.
  • Apple: Apple provides iCloud, CloudKit sharing, push-notification delivery, and Screen Time frameworks. Apple processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google: Google provides the support mailbox when someone emails peel.support@gmail.com.
  • GitHub: GitHub hosts this public website and may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy statement. This site does not add custom analytics, advertising pixels, or marketing cookies.
  • Legal and safety needs: Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect a person from harm, investigate abuse or fraud, or protect Pomise's rights and security.

6. Storage and retention

Social promises are stored in the creator's private iCloud database and made available to an accepted participant through Apple's CloudKit sharing system. Each device also keeps a local cache so the app can work reliably.

  • Promise data remains until its owner deletes it, a participant leaves, or profile deletion completes.
  • Private device data remains until it is cleared through Pomise, the profile is deleted, or the app's local storage is removed.
  • Routine support correspondence is reviewed for deletion or de-identification within 12 months after the matter is resolved.
  • Safety, security, fraud-prevention, and legally required records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Deleting the Pomise app does not necessarily delete promise records stored in iCloud. A person should use Delete Profile and Cloud Data before uninstalling when they want the complete in-app deletion process.

7. Your choices and deletion

Pomise provides controls to decline an invitation, leave a shared promise, delete a creator-owned promise, block an account, prepare a safety report, disable notifications, clear pilot analytics, revoke Screen Time permission in Settings, or delete the profile and associated cloud data.

Creator-owned promise deletion removes the CloudKit root record and its share. Ordinary participant leave ends access without silently rewriting the owner's history. Full profile deletion deletes creator-owned records and removes or redacts the departing participant's identity and contributions in friend-owned records before leaving those shares. If a cloud operation fails, Pomise keeps the local profile available so the person can retry instead of presenting a false success.

Instructions are available on the Privacy Choices page. Support with a privacy request is available at peel.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a requester controls the relevant account before acting on a request.

8. Security

Pomise uses Apple's platform protections, app sandboxing, iCloud identity, private CloudKit databases, and promise-scoped sharing. No system can guarantee perfect security, but we limit collection and sharing so less sensitive information is exposed in the first place.

9. Children

The invitation-only pilot is offered to invited adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Pomise or its support channel, contact peel.support@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

10. Regional privacy rights

Depending on where someone lives, they may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a portable copy of personal information. Requests can be sent to peel.support@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Pomise's data practices materially change. The revised page will show a new effective date. When appropriate, Pomise will also provide notice in the app.
