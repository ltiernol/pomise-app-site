The support desk

How can we help?

For challenge setup, verification, privacy, or safety concerns, email the monitored Peel pilot inbox.

Email Peel support

peel.support@gmail.com

Pilot target: safety and privacy messages within one business day; general questions within three business days.

What to include

  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and Peel version if known.
  • The challenge direction and current verification-health message.
  • A challenge ID or screenshot only if it is necessary and safe to share.
Please do not send your Apple Account password, Screen Time passcode, bank information, full contact list, or private content from another app. Peel support does not need these.

Safety and unwanted invitations

Use Report Safety Concern from an invitation or challenge when a display name, consequence, or repeated invitation is abusive or unsafe. You can also block that account and leave the shared challenge. During the pilot, the report is prepared on your device and is sent only when you choose a recipient through the iOS share sheet.

If someone faces immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate trusted person. Peel support is not an emergency service.

Incorrect or unavailable results

Open the completed challenge and use Report a Technical Issue within the displayed review window. The existing result remains visible while the other participant reviews the report. Missing or unavailable Screen Time data should become unverified rather than an automatic loss.

Privacy and deletion

The fastest deletion route is built into Peel under Your Character → Privacy & Support → Delete Profile and Cloud Data. More detail is available in Privacy Choices and the Privacy Policy.

About the pilot

Peel is currently preparing for a small invitation-only pilot. The pilot does not move money, process wagers, or send charitable payments. Support is operated by Logan Tierno Lauer through the address above.
