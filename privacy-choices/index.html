Privacy Choices

Pomise's main privacy controls are available inside the app. These instructions explain what each control does.

Delete your profile and cloud data

  1. Open Pomise and tap Your Character.
  2. Open Privacy & Support.
  3. Choose Delete Profile and Cloud Data.
  4. Review the consequences, type DELETE, and keep Pomise open until the cloud operation finishes.

This deletes promises you own. In promises owned by friends, Pomise removes or redacts your identity and contributions before leaving the share. Private Screen Time selections, monitoring data, notifications, local pilot analytics, blocks, and prepared safety reports are also cleared. If a cloud operation fails, the profile stays available so you can retry.

Uninstalling is different: deleting the app by itself does not necessarily remove promise records already stored in iCloud. Use the in-app deletion flow first when you want full profile and cloud-data deletion.

Leave or delete one promise

Open the promise and choose its options menu:

  • Leave Shared Promise removes a participant from a friend-owned share without deleting the friend's independent profile or silently rewriting existing history.
  • Delete Promise appears to the owner and deletes the creator-owned CloudKit record and share for everyone.

Manage unwanted invitations

An invitation or promise can be reported using Report Safety Concern. Blocking stores the account identifier on your device and suppresses later invitations from that account. Blocked accounts can be reviewed and unblocked under Your Character → Privacy & Support.

Change Screen Time permission

Screen Time authorization is managed by iOS. It can be changed in Settings. Removing permission can prevent Pomise from verifying an active promise, so the app should show the result as delayed or unverified rather than silently inventing usage.

Notifications and pilot analytics

Notification preferences can be changed from Your Character and from iOS Settings. Pilot analytics stay on your device unless you explicitly share the anonymous report; the local log can be cleared from Pilot Insights.
